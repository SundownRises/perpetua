3
Mezo integration

Three layers, not a token logo

  • Yield is denominated in MUSD. Every harvest is converted on-chain through Mezo's Router and streamed to stakers. lveBTC is a Bitcoin position paying a dollar coupon.
  • The fees captured are MUSD fees. 3 of the 4 live gauges on Mezo testnet are MUSD pairs — MUSD/BTC, MUSD/mUSDC, MUSD/mT. Voting fee revenue is MUSD trading revenue.
  • It directs MEZO emissions. VeBTCVoter.vote steers weekly MEZO emissions; Perpetua aims a growing bloc of that power at MUSD liquidity.

Layer 2 is not a claim in a deck — it is asserted by a test that reads Mezo's live Voter and fails if it ever stops being true.

Every new deposit deepens MUSD liquidity, because the bloc's votes point there.

6
Honesty & roadmap

Deliberate limits, and what's next

Stated plainly

  • Deposits are irreversible. Permanent lock, so no redemption. Exit by selling, like cvxCRV.
  • Operator-gated voting and harvesting in Wave 1. It cannot withdraw the position or mint lveBTC.
  • Unaudited, on testnet by choice. Permanently locking real BTC behind unaudited code would be irresponsible.

Wave 2

  • MUSDVoteMarket — anyone pays MUSD to bid for the bloc's votes, cleared per epoch. Makes MUSD the price of a Mezo gauge vote.
  • Replace the operator with an on-chain MUSD-per-vote optimiser.
  • veMEZO boost toward the 5x multiplier.
  • A real exit — seed an lveBTC/BTC pool, gauge it permissionlessly.
7
Why this, why now

A missing primitive, not another app

  • Nobody else is building it. Zero of the 48 Wave 1 submissions is a liquid locker or staking derivative — while "staking derivatives for MUSD/MEZO" is an explicit Mezo ask.
  • It is what the rubric rewards. Judging favours projects that plug into "lending markets, DEXs, vote-escrow systems" over isolated consumer apps. This is vote-escrow infrastructure.
  • Every deposit deepens MUSD liquidity, because the bloc votes there.
  • It is a lego. lveBTC is a composable primitive other Mezo protocols can build on from day one, without our cooperation.
